Frequently Answered Inquiries

Embarking on a high-caliber residential transformation requires absolute procedural clarity. Here is how we navigate key logistical, economic, and technical milestones.

Have an unusual challenge?

Our principal architects provide private 30-minute diagnostic calls for complex heritage or structural briefs.

Speak with a Lead Architect arrow_forward

We reject the industry norm of speculative preliminary allowances that inevitably expand during construction. Our estimation protocol relies on a three-tier pre-construction review:

  • Concept Tier. Parametric budget range based on square-meter architectural volume.
  • Detailed Specification Audit. Complete bill of quantities detailing exact stone cuts, timber species, lighting runs, and plumbing fixtures.
  • Fixed-Sum Execution Contract. Once engineering drawings and finishes are finalized, we lock in fixed milestone pricing. Change orders occur only if the client explicitly alters the design scope.

Occupancy depends strictly on scope scale. For single-room commissions such as bespoke master kitchens or secondary bathrooms, we establish heavy-duty negative-air zip-wall containment zones and dedicated trade entryways to minimize acoustic and dust disruption to the main quarters.

For whole-home transformations involving load-bearing steel beam insertion, full HVAC overhaul, or complete parquetry leveling, we universally recommend vacating the property for the duration of major demolition and rough-ins to ensure rapid project momentum and peace of mind.

Particularly in heritage townhouses and vintage pre-war apartments, hidden substrate decay or unauthorized historic modifications can be uncovered behind lath and plaster.

We mitigate this through pre-construction non-invasive thermal imaging and endoscope wall checks. If an unexpected condition is exposed during demolition:

  1. 01. Demolition in that zone is paused immediately, and high-resolution photogrammetry is uploaded to the client portal within four hours.
  2. 02. Our retaining structural engineer issues an engineering remediation note within 24 hours.
  3. 03. A direct, transparent cost report is submitted for client sign-off without markup penalty before any remedial work commences.
