The Falalu Methodology.

A disciplined five-phase framework engineered to deliver architectural excellence without ambiguity or compromise — from initial site discovery through white-glove handover.

Framework Governance

5 Phases · 100% Fixed Scope

Every commission proceeds through an identical architectural cadence with defined deliverables at each checkpoint.

  1. Phase 01 Vision Alignment
  2. Phase 02 Architectural Curation
  3. Phase 03 Fixed Proposal
  4. Phase 04 Precision Construction
  5. Phase 05 Commissioning
01 Step 01 / 05

Initial Consultation & Vision Alignment

Foundational Discovery & Scope Genesis

Every transformative renovation originates with careful listening. We conduct an intensive in-home or design studio discovery session to unearth aesthetic ambitions, catalog spatial inefficiencies, and calibrate lifestyle nuances against the property's historic envelope.

Phase Deliverables

  • check_circle In-depth on-site lifestyle assessment & structural walk-through.
  • check_circle Preliminary zoning, setback, and municipal code feasibility audit.
  • check_circle Aesthetic calibration monograph mapping material palettes and tone.

Duration · 1 – 2 Weeks

02 Step 02 / 05

Architectural Planning & Material Curation

Spatial Modeling & Fine Specification

Concepts crystallize into constructible blueprints. We draft millimeter-accurate CAD drawings, construct 3D volumetric interior models, and accompany you through private slab yards and hardware ateliers to specify every continuous material plane.

Phase Deliverables

  • check_circle Photorealistic 3D interior spatial renderings & lighting simulations.
  • check_circle Bespoke millwork elevations, joinery profiles, and electrical schematics.

Comprehensive Scope & Fixed Proposal

Contractual Clarity & Cost Certainty

We reject the industry norm of ambiguous estimates and surprise change orders. Our estimators construct an itemized line-item schedule defining material purchase orders, certified trade commitments, milestone schedules, and contingency allowances before a single hammer is raised.

Phase Deliverables

  • check_circle Fully transparent, line-itemized fixed-price construction agreement.

Master Artisans & Active Oversight

Execution unfolds under rigorous laboratory conditions. From HEPA air-scrubbing filtration to rigid site-protection armor, a dedicated full-time site superintendent orchestrates master stonemasons, electricians, and bespoke joiners while providing weekly photographic digests.

Phase Deliverables

Duration · 12 – 24 Weeks

While every architectural scale is bespoke, a substantial whole-home or multi-room renovation typically requires 6 to 10 weeks of discovery, spatial modeling, and permit engineering (Phases 1–3), followed by 16 to 28 weeks of active craftsmanship on-site (Phase 4). Phase 5 requires roughly 2 weeks for commissioning, zero-tolerance punch clearance, and deep sanitization.

Falalu manages the entire permitting pipeline. During Phase 2, our team coordinates with licensed structural, electrical, and mechanical engineers. We compile building department submittals, navigate municipal zoning boards, and address historic district covenants on your behalf, safeguarding you from bureaucratic strain.

For whole-home transformations, we recommend a temporary alternative residence to ensure both swift progress and comfort. For phased wing or floor-level renovations, our Phase 4 HEPA dust barriers, separate exterior tradesman access points, and dedicated temporary sanitation plans make in-place occupancy feasible and dignified.

Material procurement begins well before demolition starts. In Phase 3, we secure orders for custom imported stone, specialized European timber flooring, and specialized plumbing armatures. Goods are inventoried and held in a climate-controlled staging facility until site conditions are ready for immediate install.

We believe in absolute transparency. Any requested alteration is documented through an official change request specifying the precise dollar variance and calendar impact before authorization. No work commences on changes until signed, ensuring no surprise bills at closing.
